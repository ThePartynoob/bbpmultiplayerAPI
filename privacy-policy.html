Privacy Policy

Project: Baldi's Basics Plus Multiplayer (BBPM)
Last Updated: May 15, 2026

1. Server Infrastructure (Edgegap)

To provide the lowest possible latency, this mod utilizes Edgegap for automated server orchestration. When you join a match, a dedicated game instance is deployed at the "edge" of the network—physically closest to the participating players.

2. Information Collection & Privacy

Your privacy is protected by our server-side architecture:

  • IP Address Shielding: Unlike Peer-to-Peer (P2P) systems, your IP address is only visible to the Edgegap server instance. It is never shared with, or visible to, other players in the session.
  • Geolocation Data: Your IP address is used by Edgegap solely to determine the nearest data center location to ensure a high-performance gaming experience.
  • No Tracking: We do not use your IP address for marketing, profiling, or long-term tracking.

3. Third-Party Processing

By using the multiplayer features, you acknowledge that your connection data is processed by Edgegap to facilitate the game session. Edgegap operates as a neutral infrastructure provider and does not store your personal data beyond the technical requirements of the active game session.

4. Data Retention

All session-related data, including IP logs used for the connection handshake, are temporary. This information is automatically purged from the edge instance once the game server is shut down or the session concludes.
